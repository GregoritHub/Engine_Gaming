# Scope and limits

The delivered scenario is a deterministic finite workshop, with authored initial conditions and explicit local policies. Generated intermediate content and material effects are real within that setting. This does not establish spontaneous goals, unrestricted meaning, human psychological validity or indefinitely sustained play.

The FOL interpretation is complete for its declared finite relational signature. Its game invariants are engineering axioms, not logical truths. Unsupported legacy facets are reported rather than assigned invented meanings. Partial participant information is separate from authoritative two-valued truth.

Only approval patterns are generated by the inherited detector in the demonstrated history. The other four mature effects are verified using labelled fixtures. Supported local correction does not imply blanket clearance or free capacity. Reusable capacity requires native paid and independently observed practice; this is exercised in the engine evidence, while the initial play interface exposes local reflection.

Work and stock are finite. Native failures, zero commands, real restrictions and resource exhaustion can stop continuation. Idle scheduled turns are not credited as generated activity. No Model G pricing or replenishment is enabled. Populations two through four are the release assessment range; larger constructor bounds remain experimental.

Saves contain the full simulation and are replay-verified. They are not encrypted or a secrecy mechanism against someone reading their own file. Normal play only presents the player's received information; unrestricted diagnostics require a separate explicit inspector command. Legacy v1 field/v2 session histories remain reproducible with their documented older semantics.

Usability observations are scripted and inspectable; no human study or claim of fun has been performed. The original DOCX binary was not uploaded after automatic approval review rejected its embedded assets; the complete extracted build reference remains in project_sources.
