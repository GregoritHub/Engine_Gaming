# Directed agent field version 1

Prospective EG03. Requires accepted EG02. Use unchanged canonical CruxShellEngine with independently funded participants, not an expanded timed workflow domain. Individual C3 interactions retain their maximum of two participants; a field can connect several such interactions. No source contract is modified.

## Transport and locality

Connections are directed sender→receiver eligibility, with exact actor IDs. A public native output must explicitly address the recipient. A shared-object observation has its own directed observation subscription. Delivery records retain exact source revision, originating native operation, sender type/active element, sender position and receiver landing. Compute landing with the preserved inverse relation convention. No extra old-R21B reception charge is grafted onto a new schema: unified read and subsequent native Crux processing keep their exact costs. Transport is geometry, not proof of interpretation. Native paid reading completes before a local policy sees payload. Only subsequent paid native content work counts as transformed uptake.

Policies take detached ParticipantView and their own immutable goal/work state. No world, global heads, evaluator labels, hidden stock, peer memory or expected result enters them. Scheduler selects whose turn, never an answer or a route. The finite allocation policy may propose Theorize from an owned intention; Apply from a received model and owned stock; inspect actual remaining stock; Embody the observed remainder; then form an explicitly scoped new intention and propose Theorize. The source's individual APIs keep their meanings. Generated intention adapters must consume completed native personal outputs, retain their source, pay native binding and never receive a supplied endpoint cap.

## Scheduling and actions

Deterministic round-robin, bounded turns, positive integer work quantum; each turn performs at most one native start, advance, commit, delivery/read bookkeeping, or policy choice. Native jobs keep exact command identity and costs; no refund, repeat reward or invisible credits. Actor-local pending jobs block new cognitive work. Duplicate transmissions of one exact source on one edge do not grant duplicate input. Native revision and reservation checks reject stale/conflicting execution. Disconnection removes delivery, not unrelated world time. Unavailable/exhausted recipients cannot complete reading. The complete field wrapper keeps its own versioned state outside native checkpoints.

## Supplied setting and acceptance

Explicit workshop genesis and goals are authored inputs. Two-agent and three-agent fixtures use individually owned consumable stocks and a shared topic, no cross-owner material privilege. Match connected and severed edge runs at identical budgets, turn bounds and initial state. Check hidden undelivered world changes, arrival without reading, interrupted paid read, duplicate transmission, asymmetric edges, unavailable recipients, stale revisions and round-robin fairness. Verify exact preserved element at each landing and receiver-local C3 processing. Native auditors reconstruct paid content; a separate field checker reconstructs transmission/processing receipts from journal and access records, without importing the field policy/executor/scheduler.

EG03 proves one changed downstream choice/outcome from processed delivery. Full A→B→C→A generativity is reserved for EG05. Shell behavior is integrated in EG04; no deformation claim follows from a type label. No spontaneous goals, universal human psychology or infinite play is claimed.
